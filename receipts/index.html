Receipts to an expense sheet

Claude (free or paid) · tested Nov 5, 2026

1. Photograph your receipts

Lay them flat in good light (several per photo is fine) and add the photos to one Claude chat.

2. Paste this prompt

You're my bookkeeper. I'm attaching photos of my receipts. For every receipt, pull out: date, store, what I bought, subtotal, tax, tip, total, and a business-expense category (Meals, Travel, Office, Software, Other). Give me a table I can paste straight into Google Sheets or Excel, sorted by date. Then: 1. Total per category and the grand total. 2. Total tax I paid. 3. Flag duplicates, anything you can't read, and anything my accountant will ask about (for example business meals: who was there and why). Never guess a number you can't read. Write "?" instead.

3. Paste into your sheet

Copy the block Claude gives you into cell A1 of Google Sheets or Excel.

Tip: In our test it caught a receipt photographed twice. Do it every Friday: two minutes, not one painful evening a year.
