The AI lifestyle swap prompt

Kling Video Edit · tested Oct 30, 2026

1. Get a clip

Film yourself (5 seconds, phone on a tripod) or grab any free clip from pexels.com.

2. Open Kling Video Edit

We ran it through Higgsfield: about 10 credits for 5 seconds, ready in about a minute. Upload the clip.

3. Paste this prompt, change only the [brackets]

Keep the exact same person, face, hair, body, pose and every movement identical. Change only their world: replace [the cheap thing in your clip] with [the luxury version], replace [your clothes] with [a tailored outfit + gold watch], and replace [where you are] with [a luxury place]. Photoreal, same camera angle and framing.

4. Our exact swaps

Noodles on a bed → lobster + caviar in a crystal bowl, black tuxedo, penthouse at night. Bicycle in a parking lot → red Ducati superbike on the Monaco harbour road. Delivery bag + scooter → designer shopping bags, cashmere coat, boutique avenue in Paris.

5. Post it

Put the real clip under the AI clip, label them REAL and AI.

Tip: Name every object you want swapped. If you only describe the new place, the AI keeps your old props (our first try kept the noodle cup).
