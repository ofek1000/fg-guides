Who owns this car? The prompts

Claude + Kling Video Edit · tested Oct 31, 2026

1. Film yourself

2 minutes, phone on a tripod. Walk up to each car and put your hand on it. 5 seconds per car is enough.

2. Ask Claude to plan the owners

Here are the cars in my clips: [list them]. For each car, decide who obviously owns it (a funny, instantly readable character) and what they wear, head to toe. Give me a table: car / owner / outfit.

3. Kling Video Edit: upload one clip and paste

We ran it through Higgsfield, about 10 credits per 5-second clip.

Keep the exact same person, same pose and every movement identical, same car, same place, same camera. Change only their look: they become [owner from Claude's table]. [Outfit from Claude's table, head to toe]. Photoreal.

4. Our exact owners

Lamborghini Urus → crypto bro, oversized hoodie, iced-out Bitcoin chain, sunglasses indoors. Volvo 240 wagon → geography teacher, brown cardigan, corduroy trousers, socks with sandals. VW camper van → 70s hippie, tie-dye shirt, flower crown, round sunglasses. Rolls-Royce Phantom → British lord, tweed three-piece suit, top hat, cane.

5. Edit

Start each car on the real clip, cut to the AI clip the moment your hand touches the car. Add "who drives this car?" on top.

Tip: Name every piece of clothing. If you only name the character, the AI keeps parts of your real outfit.
